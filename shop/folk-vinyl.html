About

Folk++ is an algorithmic recombination of field recordings made across rural Malta between 2019 and 2022 — folk songs, festa band rehearsals, lullabies, and agricultural soundscapes. A generative system was trained on the corpus to produce new melodic and rhythmic structures that honour the source material while departing from it entirely.

Pressed on 180g virgin vinyl for superior audio fidelity, the record ships with a 12-page printed liner notes booklet documenting the computational process, source material provenance, and a full track listing. Each copy is individually numbered. The sleeve is printed on uncoated board with a single-colour litho overprint.
