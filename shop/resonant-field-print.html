About

Resonant Field began as a site-specific sound installation at Fort St Angelo, Malta, in which directional transducers mapped the acoustic memory of a centuries-old military space. This print captures a single frame from the parametric visualisation: a field of standing interference patterns rendered as a photographic image.

Produced as a strictly limited edition of ten, each print is made from a high-resolution C-type exposure on Hahnemühle Photo Rag 308gsm — an archival cotton rag paper prized for its deep tonal range and longevity. Every print is hand-signed and numbered by the artist, and ships with a printed certificate of authenticity.
